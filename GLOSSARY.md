# Kintsugi

A personal tracker for competitive programming practice: it records submissions, resurfaces problems you failed, and shows weak topics.

## Language

### Practice

**Submission**:
One verdict on one problem from a judge, synced or logged by hand.
_Avoid_: Run, entry

**Attempt**:
A run of submissions to one problem with no gap over 4 hours.
_Avoid_: Try, session

**Failed**:
An attempt with no AC, or 2 or more wrong submissions before the AC, or one where you read the editorial.
_Avoid_: Wrong, lost

### Problem states

**Broken**:
A problem you failed and haven't cleanly re-solved since.
_Avoid_: Unsolved, failed problem

**Repaired**:
A broken problem you later re-solved cleanly, a few days after failing it. Shown in gold.
_Avoid_: Fixed, gold, solved

**Mastered**:
A problem re-solved cleanly at every step of the review ladder. It is no longer scheduled.
_Avoid_: Learned, done

**Retired**:
A problem you chose to skip. It is out of reviews but still counts in stats.
_Avoid_: Skipped, archived, hidden

### Review

**Review ladder**:
The gaps between re-solves: 3 days, then 10, 30 and 90.
_Avoid_: Schedule, intervals, spaced repetition

**Weak topic**:
A topic where your estimated level is clearly below your overall level, with at least 3 problems behind the estimate.
_Avoid_: Weak tag, weakness
